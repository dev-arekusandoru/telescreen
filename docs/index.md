# Telescreen documentation

Your family day, on the wall.

New here? Start with [Overview](start/overview), then [Install](start/install).

## Start

- [Overview](start/overview) — what Telescreen does and what it does not.
- [Install](start/install) — Docker, HTTPS on your tailnet, and the first launch.
- [First run](start/first-run) — name the household, add people, and connect a calendar.

## Use Telescreen

- [Calendar](use/calendar) — views, navigation, member filters, and wall-display behavior.
- [Events](use/events) — create, edit, repeat, and delete events.
- [Chores](use/chores) — recurring and rotating chores, completion, and progress.
- [Meals](use/meals) — the week board, tonight's meal, and the kitchen sidebar.
- [Music](use/music) — now playing and playback controls.
- [Weather](use/weather) — forecast card, hourly view, and UV index.
- [Travel](use/travel) — driving times, traffic status, and arrival times.
- [Settings](use/settings) — household, members, modules, and appearance.

## Connect

- [Google Calendar](connect/google) — OAuth client and calendar sync.
- [iCloud](connect/icloud) — app-specific password and CalDAV.
- [Spotify](connect/spotify) — a Spotify app for playback control.
- [PantryPal](connect/pantrypal) — meals, recipes, and the shopping list.
